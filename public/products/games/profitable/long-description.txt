Profitable is a startup tycoon game for anyone who has ever dreamed of turning a side project into an empire. Start with one tiny uptime monitor and a handful of paying users. Upgrade your product to raise prices, run marketing to win more customers, and watch your user base grow while churn pulls the other way. Found new companies across Micro-SaaS, AI platforms, indie mobile games, fintech, cloud infrastructure and robotics, each one bigger than the last. Recruit eight legendary executives, from a 10x architect who supercharges your sprints to a COO who slashes every cost. Make tough calls in more than a dozen board dilemmas, ride market news and sector booms, and park spare cash on the Exchange, where assets pay dividends. When the time is right, ring the opening bell: every IPO earns permanent prestige points and unlocks new frontiers in quantum computing, space and neurotech. Your ventures keep earning while you are away, so there is always something waiting when you come back. Want to go further? The Profit Lab lets you model real-world app economics (pricing, churn, CAC, LTV and payback) and export a blueprint for your own business idea. No accounts, no internet required, and no forced ads: optional ads give you a boost only when you choose to watch one.
