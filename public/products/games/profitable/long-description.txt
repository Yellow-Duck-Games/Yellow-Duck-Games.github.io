Profitable is a startup tycoon game for anyone who has ever dreamed of turning a side project into an empire. Start with $150 and a tiny micro-SaaS, then tap to ship features and close deals while your ventures earn revenue around the clock. Reinvest every dollar: level up your products, launch marketing blitzes and found new companies across Micro-SaaS, AI platforms, indie mobile games, fintech, cloud infrastructure and robotics. Recruit legendary executives whose talents multiply your growth, cut churn and boost your valuation. Put spare cash to work on the Exchange, where volatile tech stocks, digital reserves and seed syndicates can make or break your quarter. Face tough CEO dilemmas, react to breaking market news and unlock achievements as your valuation climbs. When the time is right, ring the opening bell with an IPO and earn permanent prestige multipliers for your next run. Want to go further? The Profit Lab lets you model real-world app economics (pricing, churn, CAC, LTV and payback) and export a blueprint for your own business idea. No ads, no accounts, no internet required. Your empire is saved right on your device.
