Profitable: Venture Tycoon Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 5, 2026

Yellow Duck Games Inc. ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use Profitable: Venture Tycoon ("the game").

1. Information We Collect
We do not collect any information. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and it does not connect to our servers or any third-party service. The game works fully offline.

2. Data Stored on Your Device
Your game progress (such as your in-game funds, ventures, executives and achievements) is saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. If your device's operating system backs up app data (for example iCloud Backup or Android Backup), that backup is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. You can delete your progress at any time with the "Reset Game" button, or by uninstalling the game.

3. Sharing
When you choose to export a Profit Blueprint, the game opens your device's standard share sheet. Where the blueprint goes is entirely your choice, and we never receive a copy.

4. Ads and In-App Purchases
The game contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

5. Children's Privacy
The game is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Your Rights
Because we do not collect or store any personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any questions, contact us at: info@yellowduckgames.com

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
