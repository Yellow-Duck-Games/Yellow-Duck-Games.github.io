CHIPTUNA is a web app that runs entirely in your browser. We respect your privacy and do not collect any personal information from you when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

None. CHIPTUNA has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server-side processing. Sounds are generated locally on your device.

2. Data Storage

Your sound history and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. They never leave it, and clearing your browser data removes them completely.

3. Third-Party Analytics

We do not use any analytics tools, trackers, or advertising software.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because the app does not collect information at all.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the revised Privacy Policy on our website.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@yellowduckgames.com.
